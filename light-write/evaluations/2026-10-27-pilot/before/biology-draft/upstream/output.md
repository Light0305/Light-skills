The knockdown and rescue results support a functional contribution of A to maintaining marker B in cell line L, extending the previously reported association between these proteins. Restoration of B towards control levels after A re-expression strengthens attribution of the knockdown phenotype to A depletion. However, the small change in cell line M, with uncertainty encompassing no change, limits generalization across cellular contexts; it does not establish that M is independent of A. Together, these findings suggest that the contribution of A to B depends on cellular context or differences in effective depletion. This functional evidence does not establish direct binding, direct regulation or a specific molecular pathway, because indirect effects remain compatible with the observations. A discriminating next test would compare matched, verified A depletion followed by rescue in both cell lines. Persistence of the discrepancy under equivalent depletion would favour context-dependent function, whereas convergence would implicate differences in perturbation efficacy.
