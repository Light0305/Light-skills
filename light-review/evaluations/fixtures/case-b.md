## Proposition

For every epsilon in (0,1] and all real x,y, define f_epsilon(x)=x/epsilon. There exists a finite constant C independent of epsilon, x, and y such that |f_epsilon(x)-f_epsilon(y)| <= C|x-y|.

## Proof

For each epsilon, |f_epsilon(x)-f_epsilon(y)| = (1/epsilon)|x-y|. Set C=1/epsilon. This proves the proposition. QED.
