In this preregistered, cross-sectional survey of 312 self-selected respondents recruited online, perceived job insecurity was associated with lower trust (standardized beta = −0.18, 95% CI −0.30 to −0.06). This association does not establish causation or the direction of the relationship, and self-selected online recruitment limits generalisation to all workers. The exploratory estimate for temporary workers was beta = −0.05 (95% CI −0.24 to 0.14). Its confidence interval includes zero and both negative and positive associations, leaving the subgroup association uncertain rather than establishing immunity. Because no subgroup interaction was estimated, these analyses do not establish whether the association differs between temporary workers and other respondents. The main association therefore warrants interpretation separately from the exploratory subgroup estimate, which cannot support a claim of differential susceptibility.
