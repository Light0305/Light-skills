In a retrospective sample of 620 adults from one hospital, the model predicted deterioration with an internal-test AUROC of 0.89 (95% CI 0.85–0.93) and sensitivity of 0.71 at specificity of 0.80; calibration was not evaluated. These findings establish discrimination within the internal test sample, but without prospective evaluation, an external cohort or treatment-impact analysis, they do not establish generalisability, prevention of deterioration or readiness for bedside implementation.
