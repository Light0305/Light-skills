Community structure and measured soil pH across 24 lichen-pasture sites. (a) Principal-component scores from all 44 species, after Hellinger transformation (square root of each cover divided by its site total), column centering and no variance scaling. Axes share a distance scale; percentages give fractions of total transformed-data variance (PC1, 40.0%; PC2, 21.7%). Axis signs are arbitrary. Nearby points have similar transformed compositions in this two-dimensional projection; the remaining 38.3% of variation is not shown. Numbers retain source site IDs; colour encodes the predeclared continuous soil pH (2.7–3.6), with higher pH mostly toward positive PC1 but overlapping compositions. (b) Original estimated cover for the 12 species with the largest cover sums across all sites (ties resolved alphabetically), ordered by decreasing total cover. Sites follow increasing PC1. The colour scale uses a square-root transform with ticks in the supplied cover scale; species names are source abbreviations. All 44 species and all sites remain in the analysis and numerical exports. No groups, tests, causal effects or extraction-method/chemical-unit claims are inferred. Source: vegan varespec/varechem, Väre, Ohtonen & Oksanen (1995), supplied original objects and tables.
