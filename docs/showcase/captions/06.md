**0.995-sigma air temperature over the supplied 2013–2014 reanalysis domain.**
**a,b,** Calendar-year mean air-temperature fields for 2013 and 2014, respectively, on a common absolute temperature scale. **c,** Grid-cell mean difference, 2014 minus 2013, on a zero-centered scale; negative grid cells are hatched so that the sign remains identifiable in grayscale. **d,** Calendar-month domain means for each year, with black solid circles for 2013 and orange dashed triangles for 2014. Lines connect the twelve monthly summaries; they are not a fitted or smoothed curve.

All 2,920 equally spaced six-hourly records from 2013-01-01 00:00 through 2014-12-31 18:00, and all 25×53 supplied grid points (75–15° N, 200–330° E), are retained. Each year contains 1,460 records; calendar months contain 112, 120 or 124 records. Annual and monthly fields are arithmetic means of the corresponding source records. Temperatures are converted from kelvin by °C = K − 273.15. The domain mean uses cos(latitude) weights replicated over longitude and normalized across finite cells. These are regular-grid working area weights, not exact polygon-area weights or a population mean. Every grid value is finite, so valid weighted coverage is 100% at each time and for every aggregate.

Maps use a rectangular longitude–latitude degree plane, which is neither equal-area nor distance-preserving. Western display longitude equals native longitude minus 360°; the original columns remain paired with their values. Descending native latitude and its data rows are reversed together for ascending display coordinates. Each colored cell represents its native grid-center value and spans halfway to adjacent centers; the exterior half-cell edges are a display convention, not additional observations. No grid interpolation, spatial smoothing, missing-cell imputation or time smoothing is used. Coastlines are the supplied Natural Earth 1:110m version 4.1.0 geographic line data (EPSG:4326), clipped to the figure domain for context; they do not indicate the resolution of the temperature data.

The source is the NCEP/NCAR reanalysis air-temperature subset distributed by the pinned xarray-data repository (commit a35297e9da2cc99c811014f0c8a4297345a5c28d). The documented level is sigma 0.995, not a 2-m or 850-hPa field. Reanalysis values are spatially and temporally correlated model-assimilation output, not independent station samples. The two-year difference is descriptive and does not estimate a long-term climate-normal anomaly, trend, attribution or statistical significance. No confidence intervals or p values are shown. Provider and redistribution boundaries are retained in the accompanying source records; no new blanket license is asserted for the reanalysis data. Natural Earth map data are public domain under their supplied terms.
