# Reported-data redesigns

Source: Chen Liu et al., supplied figures4papers snapshot (CC BY-NC 4.0). These reproduce repository-reported aggregates, not independently verified experimental data.

**01. Method comparison.** All nine IEDB and ten Cancer methods are shown in source order. Point estimates and symmetric errors reproduce the source mean and std arrays. AUROC and AUPRC use literal std entries; Mean PPVn errors were already divided by √5 in the source. The snapshot does not specify independent sampling units or justify reinterpreting these as confidence intervals. ImmunoStruct is highlighted for navigation; Cancer AUROC ties BigMHC-IM at 0.771, not a unique win.

**02. Component ablation.** All 12 IEDB variants are retained. V01–V11 and Full follow source ordering; Filled circles mean included and open circles excluded in each 5-bit design. S = Structure; Q = Sequence; B = Bchems (source term); M = MMA; T = Transfer Learning. Bottom panels retain all eight coefficient conditions, both source boolean flags and all three metrics. The flag is labeled with/without transfer, inferred from source plotting labels for indices 6, 4 and 0; the coefficient is similarly interpreted as contrastive weight. Confirm these mappings with the data owner for research reuse. The symlog coefficient axis retains zero and actual nonzero coordinates. Error conventions match 01.

**03. RNA optimization.** Every one of the 13 supplied methods and six tasks is retained. Signed prediction changes use a common zero-centered scale; target-direction fractions use a common 0–100% scale. The + and − columns indicate desired directions, not test significance. Separators follow method families in the source order. No uncertainty or tests are inferred. Exact source values are in 03_structured_heatmap_values.csv and rnagenscape.json; cell numbers are omitted from the image to reduce duplicate visual encoding.
