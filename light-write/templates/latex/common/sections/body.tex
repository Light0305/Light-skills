\section{解释与边界}

正式交付前应移除这些模板说明，并明确列出仍待作者补充的信息。

\section{来源与引文}

\section{Interpretation and scope}
Present supported interpretation, competing explanations, and material
limitations. Match conclusions to the evidence; an association is not
causation, and a planned validation is not a completed finding. Remove
template instructions before manuscript delivery and flag any missing
evidence or author decisions.

\section{Sources and citations}
Keep important claims traceable to author materials or adopted literature.
Preserve citation keys, their positions, and the scope of support during
revision. The bibliography file is deliberately empty: do not invent authors,
titles, or reference numbers to demonstrate a citation style. Supplied
references are not automatically verified sources. Mark missing support
clearly and distinguish it from evidence-backed prose. A successful
bibliography build establishes that citations resolve, not that their
scientific interpretation is correct.

\section{Source files and revisions}
Maintain the title, authors, and affiliations in the metadata file, with the
abstract and body in section files. Split sections when useful and organize
them around one authoritative manuscript source. Avoid maintaining divergent
copies in several formats. A major revision or journal change should retain
the reviewed version and its adopted figure relationships. Continue the
template and column choice already settled for the project; another language
edit does not require choosing the layout again.

\section{Compilation and page review}
Deliver the editable source project and the PDF actually compiled from it.
Check citations, cross-references, equation numbers, and required characters.
An old PDF remaining on disk does not establish that the current revision
built successfully. Inspect rendered pages for complete title and abstract
blocks, readable columns, unclipped tables and equations, and unobstructed
headers and page numbers. Recompile and inspect affected pages after a layout
fix. The placeholder content in this preview must be replaced before it can
be delivered as a completed manuscript.
