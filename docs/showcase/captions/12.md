Observed overall survival and follow-up support in the supplied colon trial data. Lines show unadjusted Kaplan–Meier estimates for observation (Obs, n=315), levamisole (Lev, n=310), and levamisole plus fluorouracil (Lev+5FU, n=304); colours and line types identify the same groups throughout. Shaded bands are 95% pointwise log-log transformed Greenwood confidence intervals; plus signs mark censoring times. The death endpoint is filtered exactly as etype=2, one record per original participant, with event=status==1 and follow-up in days. All 929 participants are retained, including those with missing unrelated covariates (452 deaths overall); nodes/node4 are unused. The risk counts use these same participant records and count observed time ≥ each tick, immediately before events or censoring at that time. Curves retain the full observed follow-up through 3,329 days; only 17 participants remain at risk at 3,000 days, so late tails have limited support. The figure describes observed patterns without hazard ratios, hypothesis tests, adjustment, clinical advice or causal treatment claims. Source: survival::colon, supplied original R object and endpoint tables.
