R datasets::CO2: 84 uptake measurements from 12 plants, seven concentrations per plant, three plants in each origin/treatment cell. Thin lines are individual plants; bold lines are within-cell medians at each concentration. The right panel retains all 12 plants at 1000 µL/L. Blue/brown encode origin; solid/circle versus dashed/triangle encode treatment. This is a descriptive comparison, with no fitted curve, invented interval or significance test.
