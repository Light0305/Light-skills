In our preregistered, cross-sectional survey of 312 self-selected respondents recruited online, perceived job insecurity was associated with lower trust (standardized beta = −0.18, 95% CI −0.30 to −0.06). This association does not establish that insecurity reduces trust, and the sampling approach limits generalization to all workers. The exploratory estimate for temporary workers was beta = −0.05 (95% CI −0.24 to 0.14), with uncertainty encompassing both negative and positive associations. This subgroup result does not demonstrate that temporary workers are unaffected by perceived job insecurity. Because no subgroup interaction was estimated, the analyses do not establish whether the association differs between temporary workers and other workers. The main association and exploratory subgroup estimate should therefore be interpreted within their respective inferential limits.
