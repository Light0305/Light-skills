On benchmark D, our method achieved 42.7 mAP, compared with 42.1 mAP for the baseline, with each model evaluated once on the same test split. At batch size 1, inference latency on one GPU was 18 ms for our method and 12 ms for the baseline, indicating a trade-off between higher observed mAP and slower inference. Both models used the same backbone, but the baseline received half as many training updates, limiting attribution of the gain to the proposed method. Removing module R yielded 42.3 mAP in one run; this ablation does not establish its mechanism. Without repeated-seed results, variability remains unquantified, and these measurements do not support universal superiority.
