Descriptive sample organization and within-cell-line expression changes in the airway dataset. a, Frozen PCA coordinates of all eight samples using 16,139 eligible genes. Lines join the untreated and dexamethasone sample from each of four cell lines. Color and marker shape identify cell line; open and filled symbols indicate untreated and dexamethasone samples, respectively. PC1 and PC2 account for 37.34% and 24.00% of the input variance. The PCA centers logCPM per gene without variance scaling. b, All four paired differences for each of the 12 prespecified most-variable eligible genes, in their frozen variance-rank order. Source gene symbols and stable Ensembl gene IDs are retained. Points use the same cell-line colors/shapes as a; their small vertical offsets prevent overlap and carry no numeric meaning. Black vertical ticks indicate the median across four pairs, and the vertical reference line is zero change. No interval or significance test is shown.

CPM equals count divided by the sample’s full-assay library total, multiplied by 10^6; logCPM = log2(CPM + 0.5). Eligibility requires count ≥10 in at least four of the eight samples. The 12 detailed genes are selected by sample variance of logCPM among eligible genes, with stable-ID tie breaking; this is an exploratory variability selection, not differential-expression significance or a search for the largest treatment response. All coordinates, gene identities, normalization and pair differences were frozen before drawing. The four paired cell lines are the biological units; eight samples are not eight independent treatment replicates. Normalization remains compositional, and the figure establishes neither causal, pathway nor clinical claims. Sample IDs and pair mapping, full counts/transform arrays, eligible-gene matrix and selection flags accompany the figure. Source: Bioconductor airway 1.32.0, GSE52778, DOI 10.18129/B9.bioc.airway.
