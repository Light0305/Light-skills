In our preregistered, cross-sectional survey of 312 self-selected respondents recruited online, perceived job insecurity was associated with lower trust (standardized beta = −0.18, 95% CI −0.30 to −0.06). This association does not establish that insecurity reduces trust, and the sampling approach limits generalization to all workers. The exploratory estimate for temporary workers was smaller in magnitude but uncertain (beta = −0.05, 95% CI −0.24 to 0.14). Its confidence interval includes both negative and positive associations, so it does not support the conclusion that temporary workers are immune to the association. Because no subgroup interaction was estimated, these analyses cannot establish whether the association differs between temporary workers and other workers. The main association and exploratory subgroup estimate therefore warrant distinct interpretations.
