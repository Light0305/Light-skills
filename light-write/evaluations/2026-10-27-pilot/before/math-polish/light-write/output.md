The proved result is pointwise convergence in θ: for each fixed θ and every ε > 0, there exists an integer N(ε) such that, for all n ≥ N(ε), |a_n − L| < ε. Here N(ε) is chosen for the fixed parameter and is not asserted to work uniformly over θ. The proof supplies neither a convergence rate nor an N independent of ε. A uniform rate over θ remains conjectural. Thus, the conclusion establishes convergence for each fixed θ, but does not establish uniform convergence or a computable convergence bound.
