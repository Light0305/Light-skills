# Life sciences

- **ID:** life-sciences
- **Maturity:** draft
- **Scope:** Experimental and observational biological research, including molecular/cellular studies, organismal work, and ecological evidence as supplied.
- **Out of scope:** Universal biological reporting rules, human clinical standards, or current Science/Cell/Nature requirements.
- **Provenance:** Editorial starting guide based on general evidence-aware writing; no field-expert review or corpus validation.
- **Last reviewed:** Not reviewed

## Argument and evidence load

Keep observation, experimental perturbation, proposed mechanism, and cross-system generalization distinct. Define the biological system and level at which a result is supported. Evidence in a model organism, cell line, or controlled environment does not automatically establish the same effect in another organism or setting.

## Writing decisions

- **Introduction:** Narrow from the biological question to the specific unresolved relationship or mechanism. Connect the experimental design to that question without treating an untested explanatory model as established.
- **Methods:** Preserve organism, strain, cell type, biological material, intervention, controls, units of replication, measurement, and analysis details as provided. Technical measurements and independent biological replicates are not interchangeable; flag ambiguity instead of selecting a sample count.
- **Results:** Organize the evidence chain by question or inference. A change after perturbation supports a result under tested conditions; whether it supports necessity, sufficiency, direct interaction, or a mechanism depends on the actual design and alternative explanations.
- **Controls:** State control findings and contrary evidence that change interpretation. Do not invent rescue experiments, validation assays, randomization, blinding, or independent replication because they would strengthen a narrative.
- **Discussion:** Separate a plausible mechanism from demonstrated steps. State the system-specific boundary, competing explanations, and what would discriminate them. Do not generalize in vitro findings to organismal or clinical benefit without supplied evidence.
- **Language:** Preserve species, gene/protein names and formatting conventions from authoritative author material. Do not silently merge similar identifiers or replace an assay-specific readout with a broader biological endpoint.

## Common failure modes

| Failure | Corrective check |
|---|---|
| Technical repeats inflate biological sample size | What is the independent experimental unit? |
| Expression correlation becomes regulation or direct binding | What experiment supports the stronger relation? |
| A model-system result becomes clinical benefit | In which system and conditions was the outcome measured? |
| Missing control is narrated as performed | Is the control present in supplied records? |

## Interactions

Use clinical-health when the manuscript studies human outcomes, computing for computational inference, or physical-sciences-engineering for instrumentation. Choose conventions for the actual evidence form rather than applying a single biological paper template.
