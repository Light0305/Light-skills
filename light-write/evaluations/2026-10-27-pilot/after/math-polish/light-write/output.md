For each fixed value of the parameter \(\theta\), we prove that for every \(\varepsilon>0\) there exists an integer \(N(\varepsilon,\theta)\) such that, for all \(n\geq N(\varepsilon,\theta)\), \(|a_n-L|<\varepsilon\). Here the notation makes explicit the permitted dependence of the threshold on the fixed parameter; it does not strengthen the quantifier order. The result establishes pointwise convergence in \(\theta\), without providing a convergence rate or a computable convergence bound. A uniform rate over \(\theta\) remains conjectural. Thus, the proved conclusion is convergence for each fixed parameter, rather than uniform convergence with a computable bound.
