R datasets::ChickWeight: 578 observed weights from 50 chicks. Panels a–d retain all observed individual paths (thin) and age-specific medians (bold), not confidence bands. Panel e includes 45 chicks observed at days 0 and 21, with all complete-pair gains and medians; no unobserved endpoint is imputed. Panel f is a discrete observed-age × diet matrix of actual observation counts; each cell is annotated, not a survival probability or an interpolated continuous time field. Diet color is stable; counts also use line/marker redundancy. Descriptive only; incomplete follow-up limits comparisons.
