For each fixed parameter \(\theta\), the proof establishes convergence: for every \(\varepsilon>0\), there exists an integer \(N(\varepsilon,\theta)\) such that \(|a_n(\theta)-L(\theta)|<\varepsilon\) for all \(n\geq N(\varepsilon,\theta)\). The threshold may depend on both \(\varepsilon\) and \(\theta\), and the proof provides no convergence rate. We conjecture that a uniform rate holds over \(\theta\), but this remains unproved. In particular, the argument does not establish a threshold independent of \(\theta\) or a computable convergence bound. The established conclusion is therefore pointwise convergence for each fixed \(\theta\); neither uniform convergence nor a uniform convergence rate has been demonstrated.
