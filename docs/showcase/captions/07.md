**Mauna Loa background CO₂: long-term level and provider seasonal component, 1990–2021.** **a**, All 384 calendar months from January 1990 to December 2021, showing the provider monthly average (solid blue) and provider deseasonalized series (black dashed), plotted at the original source decimal dates. The unit is dry-air mole fraction in ppm (µmol mol⁻¹), not emissions or flux. **b**, One profile per calendar year, retaining all 12 monthly values of the **provider seasonal component**, calculated as monthly average minus provider deseasonalized. Sequential shading denotes year. The dashed horizontal line is zero; positive and negative directions also remain explicit in vertical position. Lines join adjacent supplied monthly values for reading and do not represent new temporal interpolation, smoothing or a newly fitted trend. The May maximum and September–October minimum describe this supplied provider component, not a climate-normal anomaly.

The 384 monthly slots and 32 yearly profiles are serial descriptive records, not independent experimental replicates. All original day counts, standard deviations of days, monthly-mean uncertainties and availability/interpolation flags are retained in the source and trace files. Day-to-day variability (`std_of_days`) and the provider uncertainty of the monthly mean are distinct fields; neither is plotted or converted to a confidence interval. NOAA marks provider-interpolated months by negative variability/uncertainty fields; none occur within the selected window. Negative source flags remain unchanged in the full original file and are never drawn as negative error bars. The complete preselected window ends before the November 2022 interruption and later Maunakea substitution. No other station, fitted confidence interval, hypothesis test or causal attribution is introduced.
